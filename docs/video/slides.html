SafeNight

Go out together. Get home together.
The problem

A night out has a gap nobody owns.

  • Friends split up across exits, bars, and rides.
  • The designated driver has a drink. Who says something?
  • Group chats go quiet. Nobody knows who is handling it.
  • A location pin tells you where someone is, not that anyone is coming.
Who it is for

Groups of friends, and the people they trust.

Students and young adults

Parties, bars, concerts, festivals, campus events.

Designated drivers

A role you accept on purpose, and can hand back without judgment.

Safety buddies

Named people who agreed to be asked, in an order you chose.

The loop

Plan the night. Confirm everyone is home.

A temporary Night Out group that expires. Not a tracker. Not a dispatcher.

What makes it trustworthy

Consent first. Humans decide.

  • Sharing is opt-in, scoped per person, time-limited, and revocable.
  • No intoxication detection. No one is ever declared sober or safe to drive.
  • Every outcome is confirmed by a person: booked, picked up, home.
  • The agent uses real tools, but never acts alone or outside permissions.
  • Anything simulated is labeled, in the data and on screen.
Why it is different

Not a pin on a map. A plan with people in it.

Explicit driver acceptance

Someone volunteers, then confirms. Nobody is assigned silently.

Immediate deterministic warning

A concern about the driver shows "Do not drive" instantly, before any AI runs.

An agent that adapts

Asks the right buddy, waits, escalates to the backup, proposes a ride, and hands off when stuck.

Technical · deterministic safety engine

Rules first. Then the agent.

  • Concern about the driver → incident + "Do not drive" alert in one transaction.
  • Locations are visible only through consent-scoped views; approximate scope rounds coordinates.
  • "I'm okay" never closes an open incident. Only the person confirms home.
  • Missed check-ins, expiries, and retention run on schedule tables inside the database.
Technical · Nemotron agent

A scheduled procedure that plans with tools.

  • Wakes on an incident event, reads only authorized context, calls NVIDIA NIM: nemotron-3.5-super.
  • Allowlisted tools; results fed back for up to three rounds within one plan version.
  • Every call validated against participants, consent, templates, and plan version before it runs.
  • Receipts label the source: model, policy, or simulated. Reasoning text is never stored.
  • Follow-up timers and escalation live in SpacetimeDB; nonresponse wakes the agent again.
